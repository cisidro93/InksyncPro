Continuous Audio Architecture

Synchronized reading with automated page advancement

Listen to your documents while reading along visually. InkSync Pro highlights spoken text line-by-line and turns pages automatically as playback progresses, keeping you immersed without touching the screen.

  • Continuous Page Turns: Audio proceeds uninterrupted across page turns and chapter transitions with zero playback pause.
  • Natural Neural Voices: Automatically selects Apple's highest quality on-device neural voices with human cadence and sentence pauses.
  • Adjustable Speed Controls: Fine-tune playback velocity from 0.5× up to 2.5× with pitch preservation for comfortable comprehension.

Integrated Notebooks

Structured Cornell notes with Apple Pencil inking

Every document connects directly to an integrated multi-page study notebook organized around the proven Cornell 3-Zone methodology.

  • Three-Zone Layout: Dedicated columns for Cues, Notes, and Summaries encourage active recall and clear information architecture.
  • Apple Pencil Pro Inking: Low-latency vector drawing layers with palm rejection, 1-finger drawing, and 2-finger navigation.
  • Direct Book & Markdown Export: Save completed notes directly back into your library as a new PDF/EPUB, or export clean Markdown files.

Design Philosophy

A Focused Alternative

How InkSync Pro approaches digital reading compared to conventional document viewers.

Conventional Document Viewers

  • Audio stops abruptly at each page boundary with disconnected text tracking
  • External note-taking requires constant split-screen app switching
  • Organizing series requires nested, multi-tap menus and complex folders
  • Visible flashes and layout shifts during page curl transitions
  • Reading activity and library metadata uploaded to third-party servers
  • Large multi-gigabyte file duplication required to assemble collections
VS

InkSync Pro

  • Continuous hands-free narration that turns pages automatically with line highlights
  • Integrated Cornell notebooks with Apple Pencil handwriting alongside your document
  • 1-tap volume assignment pills with instant 0 MB virtual omnibuses
  • Fluid 120Hz ProMotion touch response and Metal graphics acceleration
  • 100% on-device private processing with zero accounts or external tracking
  • Zero-byte virtual volumes that organize your library without wasting disk space
